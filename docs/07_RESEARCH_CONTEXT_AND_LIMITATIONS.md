# Research Context and Limitations

## Project research context supplied by the student
The student’s earlier project research included:
- Interview finding: Block 34 lift was reported not to serve all floors, raising floor coverage concerns.
- Interview finding: Block 33 was reported to have no lift access.
- Interview finding: Directional signs locating accessible ramps were reported as unclear, making barrier-free routes difficult to locate.

These are limited project findings, not a campus-wide accessibility audit. Keep interview findings separate from direct observations. Do not invent quotations, participant identities, dates, exact floor numbers, or unverified physical facts. If the team cannot verify exact wording, paraphrase cautiously and label it as a reported research finding.

## Public LPU information checked during project research
Official LPU pages make broad statements about accessibility infrastructure:
- LPU Disability Support Office: https://www.lpu.in/disability-support-office.php
- LPU Infrastructure: https://www.lpu.in/about-lpu/infrastructure.php
- LPU Annual Report 2024: https://www.lpu.in/sustainable/doc/annual-report-2024.pdf

The public pages describe ramps, lifts/elevators, accessible pathways, accessible washrooms, and disability support. The infrastructure page states lifts are in over 80% of campus buildings and ramps in every block; the Disability Support Office page makes broader statements about lifts and ramps across academic/residential blocks.

## How to use this context
- Do not claim LPU has no accessibility infrastructure.
- General availability does not prove a particular lift serves every floor or that a particular ramp is easy to locate or unobstructed.
- The public sources do not provide a detailed, current block-by-block and floor-by-floor directory, live equipment status, or temporary obstruction reports.
- This gap supports a prototype that organizes user-reported location-specific information.
- Clearly distinguish official general statements from student research findings and user-submitted reports.
- If the team later gets verified information from the Disability Support Office, it can be added as a separately labelled verified source with date and attribution.

## Suggested future validation
Contact LPU Disability Support Office to request:
- building-wise lift availability and floors served;
- ramp and accessible entrance locations;
- accessible washroom locations;
- a current official accessibility map/directory;
- preferred channel for reporting temporary access barriers.

Do not imply that the prototype is connected to this office.
