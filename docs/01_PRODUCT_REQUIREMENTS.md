# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## Product
**Name:** AccessPath  
**Type:** Campus accessibility information and issue-reporting prototype  
**Primary users:** Students, visitors, and people who want to check user-reported accessibility barriers before visiting a campus building.

## Problem context
The project research surfaced concerns about accessibility information and barriers, including reported lift floor coverage concerns at Block 34, student interview findings indicating absence of a lift at Block 33, and difficulties identifying accessible ramp locations due to unclear directional signs. These are project research findings, not a complete audit of campus infrastructure. The app should help people find and share location-specific reports without claiming to be an official or verified campus directory.

## Problem statement
Students may not have clear, up-to-date, location-specific information about accessibility barriers before moving to a campus building. When they encounter an issue, there may not be a simple shared place in this prototype to record the location and describe what happened.

## Product goal
Make it easy to:
- Search a building/block and discover relevant user-submitted reports.
- Understand the issue, its reported date, and current demo status.
- Submit a structured report with an optional photo.
- Distinguish sample/demo information from real user submissions.

## Out of scope
- Full LPU map or campus-wide route planner.
- GPS, indoor positioning, live tracking, or navigation.
- Real-time lift status or sensor integration.
- Official LPU database or verified facilities directory.
- Real university complaint escalation or staff notifications.
- Authentication, cloud sync, or public deployment.
- Guaranteeing that an unreported location is accessible.

## Success criteria for prototype
- A user can search by block name, alias, or issue keyword.
- A user can submit a report in under a minute.
- The report is saved locally and appears in search and My Reports.
- Demo moderator can update status and see a status history.
- User understands reports are unverified and local-only.
- Core flows work on mobile and desktop.
